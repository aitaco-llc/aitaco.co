## ARTICLE III - CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

### 3.1 Initial Capital Contribution
The Member has made an initial capital contribution to the Company in the amount of $100.00 in cash and/or property.

### 3.2 Additional Contributions
No Member shall be required to make any additional capital contributions to the Company beyond the initial capital contribution unless agreed to in writing.

### 3.3 No Interest on Capital
No interest shall be paid on any capital contribution.

## ARTICLE IV - ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

### 4.1 Allocations
All profits, losses, deductions, and credits of the Company shall be allocated to the Member in accordance with the Member's percentage ownership interest.

### 4.2 Distributions
Distributions shall be made to the Member at such times and in such amounts as determined by the Member, subject to the limitations set forth in the Act.

### 4.3 Tax Distributions
The Company may make distributions to the Member to enable the Member to pay taxes on income allocated to the Member from the Company.

## ARTICLE V - MANAGEMENT

### 5.1 Management by Member
The Company shall be member-managed. The Member shall have full and complete authority, power, and discretion to manage and control the business, affairs, and properties of the Company, to make all decisions regarding those matters, and to perform any and all other acts or activities customary or incident to the management of the Company's business.

### 5.2 Binding Authority
The Member shall have the authority to bind the Company in all matters in the ordinary course of business.

### 5.3 Duties
The Member shall devote such time to the business and affairs of the Company as the Member deems necessary and appropriate.

### 5.4 Standards of Conduct
The Member shall discharge duties as a member in good faith, with the care an ordinarily prudent person in a like position would exercise under similar circumstances, and in a manner the Member reasonably believes to be in the best interests of the Company.

## ARTICLE VI - ACCOUNTING AND RECORDS

### 6.1 Fiscal Year
The fiscal year of the Company shall be the calendar year.

### 6.2 Books and Records
The Company shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of the Company's business and affairs at the Company's principal place of business.

### 6.3 Tax Returns
The Company shall file all required federal, state, and local tax returns. The Company shall elect to be taxed as a disregarded entity for federal income tax purposes, unless otherwise determined by the Member.

### 6.4 Bank Accounts
The Company shall maintain its bank accounts in such banking institutions as determined by the Member.

## ARTICLE VII - TRANSFERS OF MEMBERSHIP INTERESTS

### 7.1 Restrictions on Transfer
The Member may not sell, assign, pledge, or otherwise transfer any membership interest in the Company without amending this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VIII - DISSOLUTION AND WINDING UP

### 8.1 Events of Dissolution
The Company shall dissolve upon:
a) The written decision of the Member to dissolve the Company;
b) The entry of a decree of judicial dissolution pursuant to the Act;
c) The death, incapacity, or withdrawal of the Member, unless the Company is continued in accordance with the Act;
d) Any other event causing dissolution under the Act.

### 8.2 Winding Up
Upon dissolution, the Member shall wind up the affairs of the Company. The assets of the Company shall be applied and distributed in the following order:
a) Payment of all debts and liabilities of the Company;
b) Payment of expenses of liquidation;
c) Distribution of remaining assets to the Member.

## ARTICLE IX - INDEMNIFICATION

### 9.1 Indemnification
The Company shall indemnify the Member and any officers, employees, or agents of the Company to the fullest extent permitted by law against any and all liabilities, costs, and expenses (including attorneys' fees) incurred in connection with any proceeding arising from their service to the Company, except for acts or omissions involving intentional misconduct or knowing violation of law.

## ARTICLE X - GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 10.1 Amendments
This Agreement may be amended only by written consent of the Member.

### 10.2 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Colorado.

### 10.3 Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

### 10.4 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

### 10.5 Binding Effect
This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Member and the Member's respective heirs, successors, and assigns.
