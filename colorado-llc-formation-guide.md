# Colorado LLC Formation Guide for aitaco LLC
## AI Technology Consulting Firm

### Overview
This guide outlines the complete process for forming "aitaco LLC" as an AI technology consulting firm in Colorado.

## Step-by-Step Formation Process

### Step 1: Name Availability Check
**Status:** TO DO
- [ ] Search for "aitaco" availability at: https://www.sos.state.co.us/biz/BusinessEntityCriteriaExt.do
- [ ] Verify name using: https://www.coloradosos.gov/biz/NameCriteria.do
- [ ] Ensure name includes "LLC" designation

**Colorado Naming Rules:**
- Must be distinguishable from existing businesses
- Must include "LLC" or "Limited Liability Company"
- Cannot use restricted words without proper licensing

### Step 2: Choose a Registered Agent
**Status:** TO DO

**Options:**
1. **Serve as your own registered agent** (Free but requires Colorado address)
   - Must be 18+ years old
   - Must have Colorado driver's license/ID (effective July 1, 2025)
   - Must be available during business hours at Colorado address

2. **Professional Service Companies:**
   - **Colorado Registered Agent LLC**: $35/year (most affordable)
   - **Northwest Registered Agent**: $125/year (includes privacy protection)
   - **Bizee**: Free first year with formation, then $99/year
   - **Registered Agents Inc**: $175/year
   - **LegalZoom**: $249/year

### Step 3: File Articles of Organization
**Status:** TO DO
- [ ] File online only at: https://www.sos.state.co.us/biz/FileDoc.do
- [ ] Filing fee: $50
- [ ] Processing: Immediate for online filing

**Required Information:**
- LLC name: aitaco LLC
- Principal office address
- Registered agent name and Colorado address
- Management structure (member-managed or manager-managed)
- Purpose: Technology/AI consulting services

### Step 4: Obtain an EIN (Federal Tax ID)
**Status:** TO DO
- [ ] Apply online at IRS.gov (free)
- [ ] Apply by fax: Form SS-4
- [ ] Apply by mail: Form SS-4
- [ ] Required for: Opening bank accounts, filing taxes, hiring employees

### Step 5: Create Operating Agreement
**Status:** TO DO
- [ ] Not required to file with state but highly recommended
- [ ] Should include:
  - Member ownership percentages
  - Management structure
  - Voting rights
  - Profit/loss distribution
  - Meeting requirements
  - Dissolution procedures

### Step 6: Open Business Bank Account
**Status:** TO DO
- [ ] Requirements:
  - EIN number
  - Articles of Organization
  - Operating Agreement
  - Initial deposit

### Step 7: Obtain Business Licenses
**Status:** TO DO
- [ ] No state-level general business license required
- [ ] Check local requirements:
  - City business license
  - County permits
  - Professional licenses for consulting

## Ongoing Compliance Requirements

### Annual Periodic Report
- **Due:** By end of anniversary month each year
- **First report:** Year after formation
- **Filing fee:** $10 (online at Colorado SOS website)
- **Example:** If formed February 15, 2025, first report due by February 28, 2026

### BOI Reporting (Beneficial Ownership Information)
- **Due:** Within 90 days of formation for new LLCs
- **File with:** FinCEN
- **Purpose:** Anti-money laundering compliance
- **Required info:** Beneficial owners with 25%+ ownership or substantial control

### Tax Obligations
- [ ] Federal taxes (Form 1065 for multi-member, Schedule C for single-member)
- [ ] Colorado state taxes
- [ ] Sales tax permit (if applicable)
- [ ] Local taxes
